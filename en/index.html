How it works

The order of a notebook, without the paper pile.

  1. 1

    Record the bird

    Official ring or your own number, sex, breed, hatch year and photo. The app suggests the next number.

  2. 2

    Link the parents

    Pick the father and mother from your list. Pair your birds and enter the season's chicks in one go.

  3. 3

    The pedigree is ready

    Three generations on one screen. Tap an ancestor to open its card, and share the pedigree card as a PDF.

Pedigree

Three generations at a glance.

Link the parents once and Birdario builds the rest. Every ancestor is a card you can open, and the pedigree card comes out as a print-ready PDF.

FAQ

Questions breeders ask

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Birdario doesn't connect to the internet to work; your records live on your phone. Only buying the full version needs a store connection.

What happens to my records if I change phones?

Make a backup in Settings: every record and photo goes into a single file. Move the file to your new phone and restore it.

Can I import my existing Excel list?

Yes, in Birdario's Excel layout. Export your records to Excel first, fill in your list under the same headers without changing the first row, then import it. Birdario summarises what will be added before saving and skips birds that are already recorded.

Which birds is it for?

Canary breeds such as colour canary, Border, Gloster, Fife Fancy, Yorkshire, Timbrado and Roller are ready in the list. For any other cage bird, just type the breed name.

Will it be on iPhone?

Birdario is being prepared for Android and iPhone and will be released on both stores.

Do I lose my records when the trial ends?

No. At the trial limit you can't add new birds, but your existing records stay. Get the full version and carry on where you left off.
